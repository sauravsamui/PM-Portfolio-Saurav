📘 Understanding "Fine-Tuning" vs. What We Just Did

You just changed how the AI acts, but did you fine-tune it?

  • This Playground (Inference Tuning): We changed the AI's "mood" using settings (Temperature) and instructions (System Prompt). It's like asking an actor to "read this line angrily." The actor hasn't changed, only their performance has.
  • Real Fine-Tuning: This involves training the model on thousands of examples to permanently change its internal weights. It's like sending the actor to medical school for 4 years so they become a doctor.

🌡️ Temperature

Imagine a bag of words. Low temperature (0.1) means the AI always picks the most obvious next word. High temperature (1.0) means it reaches deeper into the bag for surprising words. Too high = Gibberish.

🎯 Top-P & Top-K

These are filters. Top-K cuts off the list of possible words at a specific number (e.g., only consider the top 10 best words). Top-P cuts it off based on cumulative probability. They help keep the AI focused.
